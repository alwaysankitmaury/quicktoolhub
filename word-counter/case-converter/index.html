How to Use the Case Converter

  1. 1

    Enter or paste your text

    Click inside the large text box and type, or paste any content such as a headline, paragraph, email or code comment. The word and character counters update instantly as you write, so you always know the size of your text.

  2. 2

    Pick a case style

    Press one of the conversion buttons: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, Capitalized Case or Alternating cAsE. Your text is transformed immediately inside the same box. You can click a different button at any time to try another style.

  3. 3

    Copy the result or start over

    Click Copy Text to send the converted text to your clipboard, and a quick "Copied!" tooltip will confirm it. Use Clear Text to empty the box and begin with a fresh piece of text. Everything happens in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Title Case and Capitalized Case?
Title Case capitalizes the first letter of every word and converts all remaining letters to lowercase, so "hELLO wORLD" becomes "Hello World". Capitalized Case also capitalizes the first letter of every word, but it leaves the rest of each word untouched, so "iPhone case" becomes "IPhone Case".
Is my text saved or uploaded anywhere?
No. All conversions run locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never sent to a server, stored or shared, which makes this tool safe for private or sensitive content.
