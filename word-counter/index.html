How to use this tool

Simply type or paste your text into the box above. The word counter updates automatically and shows the word count, character count (with and without spaces), number of paragraphs, and an estimated reading time — all in real time, with no need to click a button.

This tool is useful for writers, students, and professionals who need to stay within word limits for essays, articles, social media posts, or SEO content. Use the "Copy Text" button to quickly copy your content, or "Clear Text" to start fresh.

Is this word counter free to use?

Yes, this word and character counter is completely free, with no sign-up, download, or subscription required. You can use it as many times as you like.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated based on an average adult reading speed of around 200–225 words per minute, giving you a quick sense of how long your text will take to read.
